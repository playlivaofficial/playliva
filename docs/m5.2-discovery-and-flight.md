## Contact, launch and sky flight

- Preparation reduced from 650ms to 150ms; authored kick playback from 2.1× to 3.5×. The same contact pose now occurs 570ms into the kick (720ms after Start).
- `KICK_MS === IMPACT_MS`: the previous 550ms post-contact wait is removed. Flight, multiplier growth and cashout availability share this boundary. The existing 40ms engine tick remains; animation samples the same clock. Perceived separation begins within one tick/render frame, not after a reaction pause.
- Position is time-derived, not eased toward a ground-level target. Initial forward speed is 7 world units/s, rising toward 9; initial upward speed is 14 world units/s, settling to a continuing 4-unit/s ascent. Height gain is approximately 2.06 units at 200ms, 3.84 at 500ms and 5.99 at 1s.
- Reaction lasts only the first 75ms while already moving. Then the supplied flying-panic clip takes over. No retargeting, skeleton edits or re-exported animation.
- Camera follows upward over 300ms and reserves headroom for the HUD. The island falls out of the dominant frame around 500–1000ms; mid-flight is airborne space with clouds and horizon depth.
- Body bank, passing speed streaks and depth-separated cloud parallax convey movement; streak/parallax speed responds to multiplier. Optional shake/streak/bank effects respect reduced motion.
- Landing returns directly to the established island framing and existing clip-specific grounding; it does not interpolate from a sky position through the terrain. Daze markers and impact effects remain.
- RNG distribution, fixed-point arithmetic, 7000ms multiplier curve, manual/auto settlement deadlines, idempotency, session persistence, affiliate links, consent and localization behavior are preserved.

## Remaining limitations

This is local single-player demo entertainment, not a certified fairness or server-authoritative game. True 1.00×/very short outcomes can still end before a full sky flight; outcomes are not delayed to force a cinematic. Landing is a deliberate cut to the impact scene, not a simulated full descent. Low-poly scenery, subtle daze markers and upfront model loading remain. Desktop browser emulation is not physical low-end-phone certification. Pre-existing unrelated encoding defects in legacy trust/footer copy remain outside this milestone.

Hosted CI run, Vercel Ready/Production status and exact deployed SHA must be recorded against the release commit in the final handoff; local results alone do not establish deployment success.
