# M5.3 — Final Island Crash gameplay and loading polish

Baseline: released M5.2 `332f034a03b691759b6e1a7ed3df221663a5f403`.
This is a bounded continuation, not a game redesign or M6 work.

## Contact, blast and fall

- The actual supplied skinned foot reaches the staged castaway at source **1.925s**, between frames 115 and 116 at 60fps. A mesh-surface scan replaces the older toe-to-hip approximation. The regression checks a clear gap 16ms earlier, contact at the boundary and separation 16ms afterward.
- At 3.5x playback, contact is **550ms into the kick**, or **700ms after Start** including the unchanged 150ms preparation. M5.2 used 570/720ms. `KICK_MS === IMPACT_MS`; flight, growth and cashout open on that same logical boundary.
- The renderer ticks the engine immediately before sampling the animation clock, removing the old potential 40ms interval/RAF mismatch. The independent interval remains for logic reliability. At 60Hz, displacement appears on the first rendered frame after contact; there is no intentional post-contact wait.
- Forward velocity starts at **17 world units/s**, approaching 19 (previously 7 to 9). Upward velocity starts at **18 units/s** (previously 14). The hit blends while already moving; panic flight takes over after 40ms with a 25ms transition. No idle/jog segment, keyframe edits or retargeting.
- The upward camera follow remains 300ms. Sky cruise height approaches 14.4 world units while forward travel continues; this avoids unbounded altitude requiring an implausible sub-second drop. Passing clouds, body bank and speed streaks retain multiplier-responsive movement and reduced-motion behavior.
- Logical loss still settles immediately at the predetermined fixed-point multiplier. The display and payout freeze before the purely visual fall. Fall position and initial velocity match the outgoing flight, then gravity-like acceleration reaches ground in **450–850ms**: `min(850, 450 + 75 * sqrt(flightSeconds))`.
- The castaway lands ahead, not by rewinding to the starting beach. Existing lightweight geometry is reused beneath distant landing sites; very short rounds land on the original beach. Camera/pose blending preserves the airborne loss frame and reveals the island during descent.
- Ground offsets are measured from the actual skinned crash clip at 25ms intervals. A CPU mesh regression samples every 5ms from 450–950ms and bounds ground error below 0.015 world units. Only the first 950ms of the supplied clip is used at runtime; source animation data is untouched.
- Dust/leaves, ring and optional small shake occur only at ground impact. **220ms later** the engine becomes READY once. The old 3800ms loss wait and extended dazed/crawling sequence are removed. Successful cashout's existing result timing is unchanged.

## Release evidence and limitations

The final handoff must record the actual feature/main Actions runs, Ready/Current Vercel deployment, exact matching main SHA, production probes and final measurements. Local checks alone do not close the milestone.

This remains a local single-player free-credit demo, not server-authoritative or certified wagering software. Immediate/very short outcomes intentionally cannot show a full sky flight; the RNG is not delayed for presentation. Fall duration and bounded sky altitude are deliberately stylized. The texture reduction retains acceptable actual-screen quality but the original 2K runtime texture detail is reduced. The <=3 MB target is met with local gzip; the 1.5–2.5 MB stretch target is not pursued at the cost of visible quality. Browser viewport emulation is not a physical low-end-phone test. Unrelated pre-existing footer/trust encoding defects remain untouched. No M6 work.
